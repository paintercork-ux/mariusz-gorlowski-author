What happens to us in a world where technology can do more and more? I am interested not only in what we can create, but also in the choices we make along the way. These are the questions I explore in my books and essays. Alongside my writing, I develop QR FINDER — a practical system that helps people who find lost pets or belongings get in touch with their owners. I enjoy asking difficult questions and looking for practical answers to everyday problems.
