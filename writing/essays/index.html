Essays & Publications

Big questions. Ordinary human stories.

Not every important question needs a whole book. Sometimes it begins with a phone call, a family conversation or a decision we are increasingly willing to leave to a machine.

In my essays, I explore where technology meets human emotions, habits and responsibility. I bring imagined situations into conversation with real research, without treating fiction as scientific evidence.

I am less interested in dividing people into those who love AI and those who fear it than in asking why the same technology can mean something very different to different people.
