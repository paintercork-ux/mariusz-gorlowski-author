Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This notice explains how enquiries sent via mariuszgorlowski.com are handled.

Who is responsible?

Mariusz Gorlowski, based in Ireland, is responsible for the personal information submitted through this author's website. To ask a privacy question or exercise a data protection right, use the contact form.

What information is collected?

If you send an enquiry, the form collects the name, email address, enquiry type and message that you provide. Service providers may also process limited technical information, such as IP address and browser details, to operate the site, deliver the form and prevent abuse. Please do not submit sensitive information unless essential to your enquiry.

Why is it used?

Your information is used to receive, assess and respond to correspondence about writing, publishing, interviews, speaking engagements and business proposals. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in handling enquiries and correspondence; information may also be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations. Messages are not added to a marketing mailing list and will not be sold.

Who processes the information?

The website is intended to be hosted by GitHub Pages and the contact form uses Formspree. The notification email service used by the author may also process messages. These providers process the technical data necessary to provide their services and may process data outside the European Economic Area subject to their applicable contractual and data protection safeguards.

How long is information kept?

Messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and follow up on the enquiry, or longer if an ongoing professional relationship or legal obligation requires it. Correspondence is periodically reviewed, and information no longer needed is deleted.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing of your personal information. Use the contact form for these requests. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may contact the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers, newsletter subscriptions or third-party analytics cookies. Hosting and form providers may use strictly necessary technical measures to deliver and secure their services. This notice will be updated if additional tracking or services are introduced.
