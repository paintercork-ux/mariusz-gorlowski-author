Privacy notice

Draft for review before launch. This notice describes the intended basic handling of contact enquiries on this website. It should be checked against the actual deployed hosting and contact arrangements.

Who handles your enquiry?

Mariusz Gorlowski, based in Ireland, is responsible for handling correspondence sent through this author's website. Privacy questions may be sent using the website's contact form.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, enquiry category and message. Please avoid sending highly sensitive personal information through this form.

Why it is used

The information is used to review and respond to genuine enquiries, business proposals, interview requests and publishing correspondence. It is not used to send marketing newsletters.

Handling and storage

When activated, messages from the contact form will be processed by Formspree and delivered privately to the website owner. The website itself is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. Both providers may process technical data needed to operate and protect their services. Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for correspondence and any related obligations.

Your choices

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of information submitted through the contact form, subject to applicable legal requirements. Use the website's contact form for such requests.

Cookies and analytics

The current website prototype does not include advertising trackers, newsletter systems or analytics cookies. This statement should be reviewed before adding any external services.
